Age of Sosaria

Age of Sosaria is a custom Ultima Online shard focused on meaningful progression, handcrafted systems, and a PvM-first experience with balanced, opt-in PvP. The goal is to capture the spirit of classic UO while modernizing systems where it improves long-term play and shard health.

📜 Shard Overview

Server Name: Age of Sosaria

Era Inspiration: Classic UO with custom systems

Core Focus: PvM, progression, economy, and world-building

PvP Philosophy: Allowed and supported, but not dominant

Custom Content: Heavy emphasis on original systems and mechanics

⚔️ Gameplay Philosophy

Age of Sosaria is designed around earned power, player choice, and long-term character identity.

PvM is the primary driver of progression and wealth

PvP exists in felucca as a risk/reward layer, not a requirement

Systems are designed to reward dedication, not exploits

The economy matters — gold has weight and purpose

📈 Character Progression

Stat Cap: 250

Skill Cap: 700

Skill Gain: Classic-style with custom balancing

Build Diversity: Encouraged through custom mechanics

Characters are not meant to be disposable. Your build choices matter.

🌍 World & PvP Rules

PvP Enabled: Felucca

Guarded Areas: Towns and protected regions

Murder System: Enabled

💰 Economy

NPC vendor pricing is tuned to keep gold meaningful

Player-driven economy is strongly encouraged

Rare items are earned, not flooded

Gold sinks and progression systems are carefully monitored and adjusted.

🛠️ Server Information

Emulator: ServUO 57.4.1

Custom Scripts: Yes (ServUO-compatible)

Regular Maintenance: Yes


📢 Community & Conduct

Respect other players

Exploits are not tolerated

PvP harassment such as corpse camping may be punished

Staff decisions are final

We aim to foster a long-term, stable community.

🧙 Vision Statement

Age of Sosaria exists to answer a simple question in the ServUO era:

What if Ultima Online had kept evolving without losing its soul?

This shard is a labor of love, built for players who value depth, consequence, and discovery.
